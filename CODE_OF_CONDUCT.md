# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

Daisy 采用 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)作为行为准则，适用于这个项目的所有场合：issue、合并请求、讨论、评审，以及代表项目的任何活动。

Daisy adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct, in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews — and wherever someone represents the project.

简单说 / In short：

- 友善、耐心，尊重不同的观点和经验；对事不对人。
  Be kind and patient, respect differing views and experience; critique ideas, not people.
- 不骚扰、不人身攻击、不发布别人的私人信息，不做在职业场合不恰当的事。
  No harassment, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.
- 维护者有权删除、修改或拒绝不符合准则的内容，并对违反准则的人采取相应措施，从警告到禁止参与。
  Maintainers may remove, edit or reject content that breaks the code, and act on violations, from a warning up to a ban.

## 举报 / Reporting

遇到违反准则的行为，请私下告诉项目维护者：在 GitHub 上联系仓库的维护者，或开一个只写“请维护者联系我”的 issue（不要写细节），维护者会私下联系你。举报会被认真、保密地处理。

To report a violation, contact the repository's maintainers privately on GitHub, or open an issue that only says "please contact me" (no details) and a maintainer will reach out privately. Reports are handled seriously and confidentially.

完整的条款、执行指南和处理方式以 Contributor Covenant 2.1 原文为准。
The full terms and enforcement guidelines are those of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
