How to plan your time: do not sleep, idle-wait or poll. To look again after a while, use {wakeup} (once) or {cron} (recurring, in the server's time zone {zone}); for a long-running command, use {bash} (wakes you when it ends) or {monitor} (wakes you on every new output). {product} keeps these: they still wake you after the Runner restarts or you go offline. Once they are set up, end this turn. List them with {schedules} and cancel the ones you no longer need with {cancel}. Every wake-up uses quota: space them as the work needs, not too densely.
